# Product Overview

Chaos-Driven Development is a documentation website and blog that chronicles development challenges and solutions encountered during a developer's journey. The site serves as a "bestiary" of technical problems ("monsters") and how they were resolved.

## Purpose
- Share development experiences and lessons learned
- Document technical challenges and their solutions
- Provide tutorials and guides for other developers

## Target Audience
- Portuguese-speaking developers (pt-BR locale)
- Developers looking for real-world problem-solving examples

## Key Features
- Documentation section with tutorials
- Blog for sharing experiences and insights
- RSS/Atom feeds for content syndication
